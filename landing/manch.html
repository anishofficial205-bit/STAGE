Is Manch free?

Yes, free while we launch. No account, no card.

Do I need to know 3D or CAD?

No. Start from a stage or a finished show, then click to add, drag to move and pull handles to resize.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Manch runs in your browser. Open the link and start building.

Where is my work saved?

In your browser. Download a project as a .zip any time to back it up or move it to another computer.

Can I bring my own designs?

Yes. Artwork in JPG, PNG, WebP, SVG or PDF, photos as props, 3D models as .obj, light lists as CSV or MVR, and floor plans to trace.

Does it replace CAD?

No. Manch is for planning and showing the show. Keep your CAD tool for rigging calculations and construction drawings.
